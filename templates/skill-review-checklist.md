# Skill Review Checklist

- Scope is single-purpose and explicit.
- Trigger/description makes selection unambiguous.
- Steps are deterministic and executable.
- Failure paths and fallback behavior are documented.
- Boundaries with `AGENTS.md` (and the project's contract docs, such as a specification or an architecture document, when they exist) are consistent.
- References are concise and relevant.
- Optional scripts are deterministic and documented.
- PR includes verification notes and residual risks.
